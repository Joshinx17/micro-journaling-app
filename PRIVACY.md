# MindLog privacy policy

MindLog is an offline journal. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or a MindLog server. The app does not send journal text, profile photos, or usage information to the developer.

Journal entries and vault snapshots are stored in folders selected by the user through Android's document picker. Drafts, preferences, and the profile photo are stored in app-private storage. The journal and snapshots are not encrypted by MindLog. The selected Android storage provider or another app with access to those files may process or synchronize them under its own terms.

If you choose to export or share content, Android passes it to the app or destination you select. MindLog does not control that destination. MindLog does not use your journal for advertising or tracking.

You can delete entries within MindLog, and can delete the journal folder and snapshots using your storage provider. Uninstalling MindLog removes its app-private data according to Android behavior, but does not necessarily delete files in user-selected folders. Backups, revisions, and exported copies may remain until you delete them separately.

This policy describes version 1.1.0. Before publishing, the developer must add a working contact address and host this policy at a public URL. Review the final release bundle and third-party library behavior before completing Google Play's Data safety declaration.
