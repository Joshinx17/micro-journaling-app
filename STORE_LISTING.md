## Short description

Private offline micro-journaling with human-readable Markdown files.

## Full description

MindLog is a simple private journal for capturing small thoughts, moments, ideas, and reflections without an account, advertising, or a MindLog cloud service.

Your journal is stored as Markdown files in a folder you choose. Write offline, browse a chronological timeline, search your entries, edit or delete thoughts, and export Markdown through Android's sharing tools.

MindLog also provides verified vault snapshots and recovery tools so you can preserve your journal files and restore them to a new folder.

Your journal remains under your control. MindLog does not provide cloud synchronization or claim to encrypt your journal. Protect the selected folder and any backups with your device and storage provider security.
